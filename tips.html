Tips and Corrections

Two separate doors. Both are pre-moderated: everything submitted is read by the team before anything is done with it, and nothing submitted is published automatically. Until the annex intake is built, both doors run by email. That is stated plainly rather than dressed up: the forms below open your mail program with the address filled in, and you can also write to us directly at contrasteverifica@gmail.com.

Everything submitted is treated as untrusted data. A tip becomes evidence only after the verification floor has checked what can be checked. Submitting the same claim many times does not raise its weight.

The tips desk

For leads: something happening, something about to happen, a document, a recording, a claim circulating that we should look at. Tips feed the team's source registry, and submitters who use a consistent name accrue a source-style track record of their own, the same way tracked outside sources do.

One limit, stated before you write: this desk is an ordinary email inbox, not a protected channel. Do not send anything whose exposure would put you at risk. If what you hold is sensitive, say only that it exists, and we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot do.

Interim intake: this form opens your mail program addressed to contrasteverifica@gmail.com. Nothing is transmitted by this page itself.

The corrections desk

For challenges to anything we have published: a wrong fact, a wrong date, a resolution you dispute, a source you say we weighed badly. Correction requests are answered in the open. A confirmed error is corrected, dated, and entered in the log below. Challenges to the record itself, including claims that the ledger is biased or incomplete, are logged and answered in the same place, under the process fixed on the Method page.

Interim intake: this form opens your mail program addressed to contrasteverifica@gmail.com. Nothing is transmitted by this page itself.

The corrections log

No corrections have been logged. This log is appended, never edited. Every confirmed error, every substantive amendment, and every challenge to the record with our answer will appear here, dated, in the order received.
