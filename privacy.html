Privacy and participation rules

Reading this site

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no comment system, and it sets no cookies of its own. Reading it sends us nothing beyond what any web server records in the ordinary course of serving a page.

What the intake collects

The tips and corrections desks run by email. When you write to us, we receive what you send: your email address, any name or pseudonym you include, and the content of your message, including any files you attach. There is no form database behind this site; the interim intake described on the Tips and Corrections page opens your own mail program, and nothing is transmitted by the page itself.

How submissions are handled

  • Every submission is read by the team before anything is done with it. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Tips are entered in the team's private source registry as untrusted data. A tip gains weight only through verification, never through repetition or volume.
  • If you use a consistent name or pseudonym, your submissions accrue a source-style track record, confirmed, partial, wrong, or unresolved, the same standard applied to tracked outside sources.
  • Submitted media passes provenance checks before any desk relies on it.
  • Corrections are answered in the open where safe, and confirmed errors are entered in the public corrections log. The log records the item challenged and the outcome, not your email address.
  • Submissions are never sold, and are shared outside the team only as the published work they contributed to, without identifying the submitter.
  • Source identities stay behind the house fence. We do not publish who sent a tip.

Participation rules

  • Do not send material whose exposure would put you or anyone else at risk. The tips desk is an ordinary email inbox, not a protected channel, and this page will not pretend otherwise.
  • Do not send personal data about private individuals, material obtained illegally, or anything whose possession is itself unlawful. Such submissions are discarded unread beyond triage.
  • Do not submit the same claim repeatedly or through multiple names. Repetition does not raise a claim's weight, and coordinated submission campaigns are treated as one source with an agenda.
  • Argument is welcome at the corrections desk. Abuse is not answered and not logged.

Retention

Tips and their registry entries are kept while the record they bear on is live, because a source's track record is the history of its submissions. The public corrections log is permanent by design: it is appended, never edited, and never removed from. If you want a submission of yours struck from the registry, write to the corrections desk and say so; the request and its outcome are noted in the registry, and identifying details are removed where that does not falsify a published record.
