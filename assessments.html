Publication begins as sealed calls resolve: the sealed assessment log predates this site, and its calls enter the public record as they resolve, under the rules on the Method page. The first entries will appear here on their publication dates, and no entry will ever be backdated.

What every entry carries

  • The sealed claim, verbatim, with its sealed timestamp.
  • The judgment in estimative words, with its likelihood band and a point probability inside the band.
  • Confidence, stated separately from likelihood.
  • Key Takeaways, and the reasoning below them.
  • Resolution criteria and a named resolution source, fixed at seal.
  • Key indicators to watch, and what would change our mind.
  • Competing explanations, and why they rank below the lead judgment.
  • Links down to the verification floor: the Contraste entries and primary evidence the factual premises rest on.
  • Status, in words: open, resolved, unresolvable, or annulled, with the resolution appended by a reader who did not write the entry.

Formats, labeled honestly

Not everything the house publishes is a scored forecast. Directional calls with resolution criteria are sealed and scored. Watchlists and scenarios carry no resolution criteria and are labeled SPECULATIVE, NO RESOLUTION CRITERIA, in the unscored register on the Scorecard, whose size is itself public. A label is never upgraded after the fact.
