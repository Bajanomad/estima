The covenant: nothing is ever removed from this ledger, and misses are shown at the same size as hits. Annulments are published and counted. A compelled removal, if one ever comes, leaves a hashed stub with the reason stated and the count preserved, under the redaction protocol on the Method page.

Brier scores

Not enough data. Brier scores measure the accuracy of the probabilities themselves, sliced by horizon so a good short-range record cannot hide a poor long-range one. The blocks appear at the same threshold as calibration. This house does not headline a hit rate; nobody credible does.

The unscored register

0 entries. Watchlists and scenarios that cannot be scored live here, each labeled SPECULATIVE, NO RESOLUTION CRITERIA, on the page and on every export. The register's size is public because unscored output is where a forecasting house hides its vaguest claims. Ours stays countable.

Raw data

The complete ledger is downloadable as structured data, so anyone can recompute every number on this page: ledger.json. It carries the same counts printed above, the covenant fields, the per-seat record, and, as entries are published, every sealed claim with its resolution.
